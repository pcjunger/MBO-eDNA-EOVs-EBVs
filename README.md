# Marco-Bolo eDNA-based EOVs and EBVs
Three tables generated as part of MBO WP2's work on generating eDNA-based Essential Ocean Variables (EOVs) and Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs). These tables correspond to appendices I and II of MBO Deliverable 2.4 report. The will be deposited in a Zenodo permanent repository and and in the GOOS BioEco EOV metadata portal, currently under development (https://bioeco.goosocean.org/), by the end of the project in December 2026.

### Table 2. List of eDNA-based candidate variables that could be used to generate EBVs from Tara Oceans datasets.

| EBV Class | EBV Name | EBV Description | Technology | Tara Dataset | Publication |
|-----------|----------|-----------------|------------|--------------|-------------|
| Species populations | Species distributions of plankton | The species occurrence probability over contiguous spatial and temporal units addressing the global extent of a species group. | eDNA metaB | 16S, 18S metaB, 16S & 18S mitags | Ibarlbaz et al. 2019, Pierella-Karlusich et al. 2025 |
| Species Trait | Phenology (in time-series) | Presence, absence, abundance or duration of seasonal activities of organisms. | eDNA metaB | metaB, but time-series is needed | None |
| Community composition | Community abundance | The abundance of organisms in ecological assemblages. | eDNA metaB | 16S, 18S metaB, 16S & 18S mitags | Sunagawa, et al. 2020, de Vargas et al. 2015, Ibarlbaz et al. 2019, Pierella-Karlusich et al. 2020, Pierella-Karlusich et al. 2025 |
| Community composition | Taxonomic/phylogenetic diversity | The diversity of species identities, and/or phylogenetic positions, of organisms in ecological assemblages. | eDNA metaB | 16S, 18S metaB, 16S mitags | Ibarlbaz et al. 2019, Pierella-Karlusich et al. 2025 |
| Community composition | Trait diversity - Functional composition of marine plankton (based on traits) | The functional composition and diversity (e.g. based on morphological, physiological or behavioral traits) of marine plankton over time | eDNA metaB + trait database | metaB+db, but time-series is needed | None |
| Community composition | Interaction diversity | The diversity and structure of multi-trophic interactions between organisms in ecological assemblages. | eDNA metaB | 16S, 18S metaB, 16S mitags | Chaffron et al. 2021, Sci. Ad. |
| Ecosystem functioning | Ecosystem disturbances - Harmful marine algal blooms | Abrupt deviances in the functioning of the ecosystem from its regular dynamics. | eDNA metaB | 16S, 18S metaB | None |
| Ecosystem functioning | Ecosystem Phenology - Phenology of marine spring phytoplankton bloom | Duration and magnitude of cyclic processes observed at the ecosystem level, such as in vegetation activity, phytoplankton blooms, etc. | eDNA metaB | metaB, but time-series is needed | None |
